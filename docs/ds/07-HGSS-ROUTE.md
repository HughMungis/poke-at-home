**Pokéwalker: avoiding setup is verified; declining a fixed story prompt is not.**

- **VERIFIED button-navigable — bypass the documented entry point.** Nintendo documents registration through **CONNECT TO POKéWALKER on the Main Menu**, followed by selecting a boxed Pokémon and establishing infrared communication. The main menu supports selecting New Game/Continue with buttons instead. [Manual, printed pp. 33–34](https://csassets.nintendo.com/noaext/image/private/t_KA_PDF/DS_Pokemon_HeartGold), [main-menu handler](https://github.com/pret/pokeheartgold/blob/master/src/application/main_menu/main_menu.c)
- **UNVERIFIED — a separate fixed-point story prompt and its decline controls.** I did not locate a verified script showing such a prompt. Therefore, I cannot substantiate “press B to decline the Pokéwalker prompt,” nor claim that no such prompt exists anywhere in this route.
- **UNVERIFIED — cancellation after entering Pokéwalker setup.** Main-menu avoidance does not establish cancellation inside the peripheral application. Infrared hardware requirements must also be distinguished from touchscreen requirements.
