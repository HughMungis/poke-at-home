- **`combat.py:200–202` — Reading zero HP restores full health.** With maximum HP 84, call `set_hp(char, 0)` followed by `hp(char)`. The latter returns and persists 84. Any HP read between lethal damage and death handling therefore resurrects the character.

- **`combat.py:590–593` — Monster attacks cannot create bleeding wounds.** Damage is capped by the worst-case seven-hit exchange count and 1.22 jitter; the bleeding threshold requires 20% of maximum HP. For example, at 84 maximum HP, the strongest normal hit is `int(round(84 / 7) * 1.22) = 14`, below the required 16.8. Bosses, disarming, and crowds only reduce damage. Repeated minor wounds can reach severity 3, but retain zero bleeding. The advertised combat-generated bleeding cannot occur through this path.

- **`combat.py:687–692`, `637–645` — Spirit recovery measures time since the previous recovery, not survival since death.** A character's first death reduces spirit to 9, but leaves `spirit_ts` absent. At the next nonbleeding heartbeat, `now - 0 >= 600`, so spirit immediately returns to 10. Subsequent deaths also fail to reset the timer; dying one second before an existing timer expires permits recovery one second later.

- **`combat.py:563–565` — The minimum damage defeats the crowd bound.** Every successful attack deals at least one damage, even after exponential falloff becomes negligible. With 100 attackers that all land their attacks, the character takes at least 100 damage regardless of the crowd values supplied. Total crowd damage consequently grows without bound as attackers are added.

- **`progression.py:224–250` — A concurrent gain can be overwritten by a pulse, losing earned experience.** Suppose a skill has pool 100 and absorption rate 0.045. Process A fetches the pulse rows, then process B calls `gain(..., bits=5)` and commits pool 105. A calculates from its earlier snapshot and writes pool 95 with five absorbed bits. The five newly earned bits disappear. The snapshot is read before the write transaction, so SQLite's write serialization does not prevent this interleaving.
